Comparison and recommendations

Use the comparison to identify unexplained gaps, missing end uses, incorrect operating assumptions, metering opportunities, and potential energy-conservation measures.

Measurement warning: current and voltage measurements involving panels, disconnects, or live conductors must be performed only by a qualified person using appropriately rated instruments and safe work procedures. Current-only readings should not be treated as real power where power factor or load cycling is significant.
